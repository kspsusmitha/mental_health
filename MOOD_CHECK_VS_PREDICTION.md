# Mood Check vs Mood Prediction - Key Differences

## Overview

Both features help users understand their mental state, but they serve different purposes and work differently.

---

## 📝 **MOOD CHECK** (Mood Tracking Screen)

### **Purpose:**
Record your **current mood** right now

### **What It Does:**
- **Records** how you're feeling at this moment
- **Saves** your current emotional state
- **Analyzes** your input with AI
- **Creates** a journal entry

### **How It Works:**
1. **User Input Required:**
   - Select overall mood (Very Happy, Happy, Neutral, Sad, Very Sad)
   - Adjust intensity slider (1-10)
   - Check emotion boxes (Happy, Anxious, Stressed, etc.)
   - Write description (optional)

2. **AI Analysis:**
   - Analyzes your current input
   - Detects primary emotion
   - Calculates mood score
   - Identifies risk level
   - Provides immediate suggestions

3. **Saves Data:**
   - Creates a journal entry
   - Saves to database
   - Becomes part of your history

### **When to Use:**
- ✅ Right now - "How am I feeling?"
- ✅ Daily check-ins
- ✅ After an event or situation
- ✅ When you want to record current state
- ✅ Quick mood capture

### **Output:**
- Current mood analysis
- Immediate suggestions
- Saved journal entry

---

## 🔮 **MOOD PREDICTION** (AI Mood Prediction Screen)

### **Purpose:**
**Predict** your future mood based on patterns

### **What It Does:**
- **Analyzes** your past journal entries
- **Identifies** patterns in your mood history
- **Predicts** what mood you might experience
- **Shows** trends and patterns

### **How It Works:**
1. **No User Input Required:**
   - Automatically loads your journal history
   - Analyzes past entries
   - Looks for patterns

2. **Pattern Analysis:**
   - Weekly patterns (which days you feel better/worse)
   - Time patterns (what times of day you're stressed)
   - Trend analysis (improving or declining mood)
   - Stress trigger identification

3. **Prediction:**
   - Predicts next mood based on patterns
   - Shows confidence level
   - Identifies recurring stress triggers
   - Provides recommendations

### **When to Use:**
- ✅ To understand your patterns
- ✅ To predict potential mood episodes
- ✅ To identify stress triggers
- ✅ To see long-term trends
- ✅ Planning and prevention

### **Output:**
- Predicted future mood
- Identified patterns (weekly, time-based, trends)
- Common stress triggers
- Recommendations based on patterns

## 📊 **COMPARISON TABLE**

| Feature | Mood Check | Mood Prediction |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| **Purpose** | Record current mood | Predict future mood |
| **Input Required** | ✅ Yes - User must input | ❌ No - Uses history |
| **Data Source** | Current user input | Past journal entries |
| **Time Focus** | Right now | Future/Patterns |
| **AI Analysis** | Analyzes current input | Analyzes historical patterns |
| **Saves Entry** | ✅ Yes - Creates journal entry | ❌ No - Just shows analysis |
| **Pattern Detection** | ❌ No | ✅ Yes |
| **Stress Triggers** | From current input | From historical patterns |
| **Use Case** | Daily check-ins | Understanding trends |
| **Frequency** | Multiple times per day | Once per day/week |

---

## 🎯 **KEY DIFFERENCES**

### **1. Time Perspective**
- **Mood Check:** Present moment ("How do I feel NOW?")
- **Mood Prediction:** Future/Patterns ("What might I feel based on my history?")

### **2. User Interaction**
- **Mood Check:** Requires active input (checkboxes, slider, text)
- **Mood Prediction:** Passive analysis (just views results)

### **3. Data Usage**
- **Mood Check:** Uses current input + AI analysis
- **Mood Prediction:** Uses historical journal entries

### **4. Output**
- **Mood Check:** Current mood analysis + saves entry
- **Mood Prediction:** Pattern insights + predictions

### **5. Purpose**
- **Mood Check:** Recording and immediate feedback
- **Mood Prediction:** Understanding patterns and prevention

## 💡 **EXAMPLE SCENARIOS**

### **Scenario 1: Morning Routine**
- **Mood Check:** "I feel anxious this morning" → Records it, gets suggestions
- **Mood Prediction:** "Based on your history, you tend to feel anxious on Mondays" → Shows pattern

### **Scenario 2: After Work**
- **Mood Check:** "I'm stressed after a difficult meeting" → Records current stress
- **Mood Prediction:** "You often feel stressed in the evenings" → Shows time pattern

### **Scenario 3: Weekly Review**
- **Mood Check:** Multiple entries throughout the week
- **Mood Prediction:** "Your mood has been declining this week" → Shows trend

---

## 🔄 **HOW THEY WORK TOGETHER**

1. **Mood Check** creates journal entries
2. **Mood Prediction** analyzes those entries
3. More check-ins = Better predictions
4. Predictions help you prepare for potential mood episodes

### **Workflow:**
```
Mood Check (Record) 
    ↓
Journal Entry Saved
    ↓
Mood Prediction (Analyze)
    ↓
Patterns Identified
    ↓
Better Prepared for Future
```
